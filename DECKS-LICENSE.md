# Deck licences

The plugin's code is MIT (`LICENSE`). The card texts are prose, so they carry their own licences.

## Shipped decks

The six decks in `data/decks/` (Agent unblock, Human unblock, Coding, Design, Shaders, Writing) are
by Studio Luftbrücke and licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
You may share and adapt them for any purpose, including commercially, if you credit
"Studio Luftbrücke, UnblockBrücke Cards" and say whether you changed them.

Every card is written new for this plugin. The cards follow the spirit of Brian Eno and Peter
Schmidt's Oblique Strategies; none of the original Oblique Strategies are included, and no data
derived from their text either. The maintainer checks every new card against them before it ships.

## Community decks

Decks in `data/decks/community/` are by the people named in each file's `by` line, under the
licence in its `license` line: CC BY 4.0 or
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each contributor confirmed in their
pull request that the text is their own work.

## Your own decks

Decks you write or import stay on your machine, in the plugin's data folder of your Hermes
profile. They are yours; the plugin never sends them anywhere.
